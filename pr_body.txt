This PR includes build and compatibility fixes applied to get a clean build on modern toolchains and OpenSSL 3:

- Disambiguate `filesystem` usages to `boost::filesystem` where needed
- Fixed syntax/brace errors and added bounds in `src/smessage` to prevent unbounded allocations

- Added `boost_chrono` linker flag in `src/makefile.unix` to resolve linking of `boost::chrono`
